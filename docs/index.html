A few practical questions

Before the first shift.

Is this a live product demo?

No. The walkthrough is an illustrative concept showing the workflows we’re exploring. It does not connect to restaurant systems or generate operational guidance.

Does ProvisionOS replace a manager?

No. The goal is to make your existing operating know-how easier to use. Your team remains responsible for decisions, coaching, and your approved safety procedures.

What would a pilot start with?

One repeated question or routine, an existing standard, and a small team willing to give feedback. Agree on what to measure before broadening the scope.

Where does my discussion brief go?

Nowhere unless you copy it yourself. The planner runs in this page, sends no information, and does not save your choices. It does not book or request a pilot.
